# How to play

Welcome to the night shift. You are the new security guard of the Noname Complex. Your job
is simple: get through the levels. Nobody said it would be easy.

## Controls

- **Move, jump, sprint, sneak:** your usual Minecraft keys.
- **Flashlight:** the Flashlight key, F by default. You can change it in Options, Controls,
  Every Tunnel.
- **Pick something up:** right-click it. Batteries and water bottles lie on the floor.
- **Drink or change the battery:** hold the use button (right-click by default) with the
  bottle or the battery in your hand.
- **Doors and buttons:** right-click, as usual. You cannot break or place blocks.
- **Read a note:** right-click a sheet of paper or a board. Escape, E or a click puts it down.
- **Drink from a water dispenser:** right-click it. Not every dispenser still works.

## What you keep an eye on

Four rows sit above your hotbar.

- **Health** (hearts, bottom left). They shake when you are close to the end.
- **Stamina** (lungs, top left). Sprinting and jumping use it. Run it dry and you are out of
  breath: you cannot sprint until it comes back, and you pant. The lungs turn grey.
- **Thirst** (drops, bottom right). It goes down slowly, faster while you sprint. Empty, it
  starts to hurt. Drink a water bottle, or from a dispenser that works, to fill it up.
- **Battery** (top right). Your flashlight runs on it. When it blinks red, it is nearly
  dead and the light starts to fail. Put in a new battery before it dies.

Your flashlight is built in: it never leaves you, and nothing shows in your hand.

## Hiding

Some rooms have lockers. Right-click one from in front of its door to get in. Inside you make
no noise and nothing sees you, as long as nothing saw you get in. Sneak or right-click again
to step out.

## Carrying things

Some things are carried in your hands, one at a time: you will know them when you see them.
While you carry one you cannot sprint, and you cannot put it down just anywhere. If you
die, it goes back to where you found it.

## Dying

Dying does not end your shift. The screen goes black and you start the level over, with
full health, stamina, thirst and battery. Whatever you picked up, you keep. Whatever you
used, stays used.

## Moving on

Each level has a way down to the next one. Follow the painted arrows on the floor. Some
doors stay shut until you have what they ask for. Once you go down to a new level, there
is no way back.

## A new shift

Your shift is saved when you leave. CONTINUE takes you back to the start of the furthest
level you reached, with what you carried and everything you solved. NEW GAME starts the
shift over from the elevator.

## Tips

- Take your time. Nothing down here rewards rushing.
- Supplies are few. Search side rooms, and do not waste batteries.
- Play with headphones. Most of what you need to know, you will hear.
- Not everything down here listens. Some things only watch.
- Read what people left behind. Offices keep more than paperwork.
