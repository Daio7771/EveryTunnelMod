# Noise

Down here, sound is everything. What lives in the lower levels cannot see you. It hears
you. Every noise you make tells it where you were.

## How loud you are

From quietest to loudest:

- **Sneaking** is almost silent. It is slow, but it is the safest way to move.
- **Changing the battery, the flashlight's click and drinking** are small noises. Small, not
  silent.
- **Panting** when you are out of breath carries further than you think.
- **Walking** is heard at a fair distance.
- **Jumping, and landing after it,** are loud. Long falls are louder.
- **Doors, buttons and levers** are loud.
- **Sprinting** is the loudest thing you can do, step after step.

The light of the flashlight makes no noise at all. Only its click does.

## Walls

Walls muffle sound. Every wall between you and a noise makes it carry less far. A wall
between you and danger is the best friend you will make tonight.

## Listen back

You can hear it too. Learn its sounds.

- **Bare footsteps** mean it is moving. Slow steps: it is walking. Fast and heavy: it is
  running.
- **Breathing** means it is close. When the breathing stops, it is listening. Do the same:
  stand still and make no sound.
- **Your heartbeat** gets faster and louder the closer it is.
- **The music** follows what it does. When the music changes, something has changed.

## If it heard you

- It goes to where it heard the noise, not to where you are now.
- A faint noise makes it stop and listen. A loud one makes it come running.
- If you stay quiet, it reaches the last place it heard you and searches for a moment.
  Any noise there, and it comes for you again.
- Do not let it touch you.
