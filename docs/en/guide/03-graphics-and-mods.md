# Graphics and mods

Every Tunnel works on its own: everything it needs comes inside its file. A few other mods
make it look and sound much better.

## Recommended mods

- **Sound Physics Remastered.** Echoes in the garage and sounds muffled behind walls. The
  game's sounds are made for it. Strongly recommended.
- **Sodium, Iris and Indium,** with the **Complementary Reimagined** shader pack. Real
  shadows, smooth light and wet, worn surfaces.

## With a shader pack

- The game sets up Complementary for you the first time: it turns off **colored lighting**
  (and the reflections that depend on it). Leave it off. With it on, the lamps and your
  flashlight light nothing.
- In the shader pack's settings, set **RP Support** to **labPBR** to see the surfaces in
  full detail.
- Other shader packs may work, but they are not tuned.

## Without a shader pack

The game helps your flashlight: the screen darkens around a round spot of light that
sways a little when you turn. That is normal.

## Brightness

In the levels, the Brightness option always counts as **Moody**. The dark is part of the
game, so turning brightness up will not help. Outside the levels your setting works as
usual.

## Sound settings

- Play with **headphones**: you need to hear where sounds come from.
- Keep the **Music** volume on. Down here, the music tells you things.
- **Ambient/Environment**, **Blocks** and **Players** carry the garage's sounds, the lamps'
  hum and your own breath and heartbeat.

## The main menu

Press **Ctrl+Shift+V** in the main menu to switch to Minecraft's own title screen (other
worlds, multiplayer) and back.
