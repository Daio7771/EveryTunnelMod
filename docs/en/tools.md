# Python tools

*[Versión en español](../es/tools.md) · [All pages](README.md)*

Almost all content is generated by Python scripts in `tools/`, so it can be remade from
parameters. Every script is seeded: the same parameters always give the same output.

## Setup

- **Python 3 with Pillow** for everything.
- **Sounds** also need **numpy**, **scipy** and the system's **libsndfile** (1.1 or newer,
  for MP3). On Arch: `sudo pacman -S python-numpy python-scipy libsndfile`. The `soundfile`
  package is used if installed; otherwise libsndfile is called directly.
- **The logo** needs a display serif font (Noto Serif Display was used). The PNG is
  committed, so the font is only needed to change it.

Run every tool **from the repository root**. Each writes its real output into `src/`
(committed) and previews into its own `output/` folder (git-ignored).

## Map

`tools/map/map_generator.py` builds the levels: the two of the parking garage and Level 3,
the offices.

- **Parameters** are at the top of the file: Level 1's (`HALLS`, `SIDE_ROOMS`, `LOOT`,
  `SEED`, `WEAR`, lights, props, oddities...) and `LEVEL_2`, a dict of overrides for
  Level 2 (darker, more worn, `PALE_MEN` per hall, `KEYCARD_HALL`, `END = "none"`).
  `LEVEL_3` is Level 3: every box of its plan (rooms, doors, corridors, the library, the
  bunker), heights, materials and its stand-in blocks, the hooks' spawn points.
  `MENU_SCENE` and `MENU_CAMERA` are the main menu's scene: one hall and the camera.
- **Modules** in `tools/map/garage/`:

| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `level.py` | A whole level: halls chained by corridors and ramps, the elevator lobby or entry corridor, side rooms, the exit ramp or keycard exit |
| `build.py` | One parking hall: structure, wear, props, ceiling pipes and beams, oddities, lights |
| `props.py` | Prop templates and placement rules; the aisle stays clear at body height |
| `mod_blocks.py` | Exact block states for the mod's blocks (facings, connections, stain patterns), since `/setblock` takes states as given |
| `textures.py` | Procedural 16×16 textures and the world resource pack |
| `pbr.py` | LabPBR 1.3 maps for every texture |
| `glyphs.py` | Sign texts mapped to `SignTileBlock` (facing, slot) pairs |
| `preview.py` | Top-down plan of the level and texture sheets |
| `menu_scene.py` | The main menu's scene: one hall, written into the mod with the models of its re-skinned blocks |

- **Level 3** has its own modules in `tools/map/offices/` (mod pack only):

| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `level.py` | The level from `LEVEL_3`: spaces as boxes of air, the shell round them (floor, walls, ceiling in each space's materials), the fittings, zones and spawn points; `Placement` turns and moves the plan onto Level 2's door |
| `checks.py` | A walk over every standing place from the antechamber (every room, the library, the hatch, the bunker, the door to B4; nothing left out) and a check that rooms are closed but for their doors. A failed check stops the generator |
| `textures.py` | Level 3's own textures (carpet, plaster, ceiling tiles, vinyl, wood, concrete), added to the world resource pack |
| `furnish.py` | The furniture: a template per kind of room (`FURNISH` in `LEVEL_3`), worked out in the room's own frame from its main door, kept off the doors; the library's rows, clearing and the librarian's desk round the hatch (`LIBRARY_LAYOUT`); the ring's plants and clocks; the joined states |
| `preview.py` | Top-down plan of Level 3 read from its blocks, furniture included, with the bunker beside it |

- **Level 4** has its own modules in `tools/map/lounge/` (mod pack only):

| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `level.py` | The level from `LEVEL_4`: its spaces, the shell round them, lamps, doors and steps, in two sets of blocks (the corridor, built with Level 3; the rest, built in play) |
| `furnish.py` | The furniture, the nine notes and the loot, room by room, with gaps kept clear for what the puzzles bring |
| `checks.py` | A walk from Reception reaching every cell, a wall between all rooms, three steps down to the Calm room; they stop the generator |
| `textures.py` | Level 4's own textures (burgundy carpet, walnut with a brass strip, cream walls and ceiling), added to the world's pack |
| `preview.py` | Top-down plan of Level 4 with sizes and openings. `python3 -m lounge.preview` from `tools/map` draws it alone, installing nothing |

- **Level 5** has its own modules in `tools/map/lab/` (mod pack only), from `LEVEL_5`: the user's plans at
  2/3, corridors 4 wide (the drain's tunnels 5). The three layers share the plan coordinates; layer 2 lies
  `LAYER_DROP` (10) under layer 1, layer 3 `DRAIN_DROP` (7) under layer 2, east of it but for the foot of the
  ladder; a space may stand `rise` over its layer (the director's office and the corridor out of it, 10 up its
  steel stairs). Surfaces take the look of the space they face (`STYLE_OF`, `STYLES`: the lab's tiles, the
  drain's stained concrete, steel, the office's, the garage's own blocks for the corridor, black for the pit).
  The plan turns into the world so that its way in leaves Level 4's dark stretch (made longer, 44 blocks)
  through a steel frame by its far end, west of every other level.

| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `level.py` | The level from `LEVEL_5`: spaces, openings, the flight of stairs and the steel climb, the shell round the air in each space's look (white tiles, a green band at their foot, vinyl, ceiling panels; steel and checker plate round the airlock and the frame; the drain's concrete; the office's; the garage's), lights (fluorescents; the drain's caged lamps, some flickering, some dead; the office's panels), steel doors, the airlock's doors, the grate, the office's doors and Level 1's EXIT door with its sign; the drain's ladder and its shaft up to the hatch, its channels and pools of water (placed last), catwalks and railings, the collapse, the pipes down its tunnels, its grime, the pit under the corridor's end; two sets of blocks (the way in, built with Level 4; the rest, built in play, its air included); the level file's zones |
| `furnish.py` | The three layers' furniture room by room, the fuse box, what lies about (the wrench, the detector, the fuses, the reagents, the loot), the cards, the computers, the chemical room's mixer, pipe, alarm lights and the drain's hatch; the drain's sluice gate, pumps and their pipes, the Hideout's things and the guard's note, the director's office; the spawn points of the enemies, the subjects, the figure, the alarm's enemies, the dead guard and the restart points |

| `preview.py` | The three layers one over the other with sizes, zones, openings, layer 3's water, catwalks, collapse and pit. `python3 -m lab.preview` from `tools/map` draws it alone, installing nothing |

- **Output** (`tools/map/output/`): the mod datapack `every_tunnel_level1_mod` (level JSON
  files and `garage:build`), the vanilla datapack `every_tunnel_level1_vanilla`,
  `level_resources.zip`, `level_preview.png`, `level3_preview.png`, `texture_sheet.png`,
  `level3_furniture.png` (the same, bigger), `level3_texture_sheet.png`, `level4_preview.png`,
  `level4_texture_sheet.png`, `level5_preview.png`, `level5_texture_sheet.png`, `pbr_sheet.png`. The run prints both build stages' chunks and
  commands. Into the
  mod (in git): the menu scene, `assets/every_tunnel/menu/scene.json` and the models and
  textures under `block/menu/`.
- **Install:** into every world listed in `INSTALL` that exists: `run/saves/noname_complex`
  and, if present, its dev template `run/dev_templates/noname_complex`.

The mod runs `garage:build` every time the world opens, so after running the generator,
just open the world. `/function garage:build` rebuilds by hand. See [Levels](levels.md).

## Blocks

`tools/blocks/generate.py` makes the assets of the mod's 146 blocks. The blocks themselves
(properties, shapes, sounds) are registered in Kotlin (`platform/ModBlocks.kt`); the tool
**stops if the two id lists disagree**. Model rotations must match the shapes in
`platform/BlockShapes.kt`. Textures reuse the map's painters (`garage/textures.py`), so
props look like the level around them. It also writes the block names into
`lang/en_us.json` (other keys are kept). Preview: `tools/blocks/output/blocks_sheet.png`.

## Entity art

- `tools/entity/pale_man.py`: about 2.75 blocks tall, long thin limbs, big hands, a long neck,
  milky eyes, big ears, a torn night-guard shirt. Based on
  `tools/entity/reference/pale_man_concept.png` (AI concept art, **reference only**, not
  shipped). Also writes painting templates for repainting by hand. Its `STOOP_*` numbers are
  his stoop under low ceilings (drawn in the preview, about 1.9 blocks tall); the game
  applies them in `client.render.PaleManModel`, which holds the same numbers.
- `tools/entity/guard.py`: a standard 64×64 wide-arm skin. Black jacket with SECURITY in
  yellow, badge, radio, boots.
- `tools/entity/dead_guard.py`: Night Guard No. 13 in Level 5's Hideout. A player's body (the skin's
  boxes) sitting on the floor against a wall, legs out, hands on the floor, head on his chest; the
  uniform from `guard.png`, worn out: grey skin, eyes shut, a few days' beard, grime and water marks,
  a knee through.

## GUI

Small scripts, each one texture. Masks are drawn as text in the script (`#` outline, `o`
fill...), so they are easy to edit.

## Sounds

```
catalog.py (synth, one function per sound) ─┐
cc0_cuts.py (cuts from cc0/ and licensed/) ─┼─> generate.py  -> WAV previews + index.html
                                            └─> export.py    -> .ogg + sounds.json in the mod
dsp.py: the DSP toolkit · audio_io.py: reading/writing audio
```

- `catalog.py`: each generator takes `(rng, variant)` and returns a mono or stereo array.
  Ids follow the sound events (`every_tunnel:<id>`). Mono for sounds positioned in the
  world, stereo for music and head sounds. Its newer helpers (Level 5's second pass and the
  sounds it shares) sound less synthetic than plain partials: `modal_hit` (a struck body's
  many uneven modes: `bar`, `plate_modes`, `glass_modes`), `thud`, `scrape`, `hinge`, `motor` (a shaft
  and gears turning, not a tone), `air` (gas under a falling pressure), `rail`. Use them for
  new mechanical sounds. (`ring` is an older, different helper.)
- `menu_piano.py`: the menu theme, a piano piece by the user, called by `catalog.py`. It
  has its own helpers and seed so it matches the original.
- `dsp.py`: 44.1 kHz, seeded noise (with resonant `peaks` if wanted), envelopes, filters,
  `struck()` (modes ringing from rest, driven by a force pulse whose length dulls the high
  ones, split in pairs that beat). Loops are built "circularly"
  (whole cycles, FFT-shaped noise) so they repeat with no seam; `loop_seam()` checks it.
- `cc0_cuts.py`: a `Cut` turns one recording into one sound event: segments, resampling,
  mono, fades, normalisation.
- `export.py`: the Ogg encoder stamps a random stream number into each file, so
  `export_manifest.json` keeps a hash of each file's audio and unchanged files are not
  rewritten. Git diffs stay clean.

Licences: only CC0 recordings go into `cc0/` (with their source in `cc0/SOURCES.md`).
Anything needing credit goes into `licensed/`, with its credit in `CREDITS.md`.
