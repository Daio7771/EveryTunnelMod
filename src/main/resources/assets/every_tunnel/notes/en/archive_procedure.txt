ARCHIVE PROCEDURE 7 - RETURN OF MATERIAL

Archive boxes found outside the archive are to be taken to the empty shelf in Storage. Ten boxes are outstanding.

Every box placed on the Storage shelf is recorded by reading room supervision, which will attend Storage in person to inspect it. Staff are not to be present in Storage during an inspection.

Once the archive is complete, bound volumes held in departmental offices are to be returned to the librarian's counter. Access to the lower level will be restored when every volume has been returned.
