REQUEST FOR A SALARY REVIEW

To: Management Office
From: Observation Post, Library, Level B3

I am writing to request a review of my salary.

I have held this post for two years. I watch the library from the first hour of the night to the last. I do not sit and I do not look away. That was the instruction, and I have followed it.

Since the last delivery I have been asked to stay through the night shift, and the day shift, and a shift that has no name. I was told this was temporary, and that the overtime would appear on my next payslip. It has not appeared on any of them.

I have not been paid for eleven weeks. I no longer remember when I last ate. My eyes do not close the way they used to, and I have started to feel that I am watched more than I watch.

I would be grateful for an answer before the next rotation. Until then I will keep my post. I will keep watching.

- Marcus Guard
