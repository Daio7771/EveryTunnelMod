DANIEL'S DIARY

Rowan's bed was made this morning. Rowan's boots were under it.

I don't sleep here any more. I walk the rooms and write down what I count.

Management called the desk. That phone has no line. They are pleased with me. They say I watch well.

They offered me a post upstairs, in the archives. Quiet work. A better uniform.

They left it on the chair. Black. White gloves.

It fits.
