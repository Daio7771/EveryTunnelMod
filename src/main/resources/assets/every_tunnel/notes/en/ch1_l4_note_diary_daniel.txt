DANIEL'S DIARY

Rowan's bed was made this morning. Rowan's boots were under it.

I don't sleep here any more. I walk the rooms and write down what I count.

Management called the desk. That phone has no line. They are pleased with me. They say I am thorough.

They asked me to sign off the lower labs, to close the project cleanly. I said I would, once Rowan is back.

We started this together. I am not finishing it alone.
