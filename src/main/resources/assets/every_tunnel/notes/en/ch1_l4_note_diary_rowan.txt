ROWAN'S DIARY

Night 3. Tried the suite on my break. Slept like a child.

Night 9. Stopped setting the alarm. I wake on time anyway. Mostly.

Night 14? My watch says a different day from the log. The log must be wrong.

Someone left a plate by the bed. It was still warm. I said thank you to the room.

Just a little longer tonight. Nothing ever happens down here.
