TECHNICAL SHEET - DISTORTION CLOUD

Classification: lethal. Appears without warning, already whole.

Description: a mass of black vapour, never still, with a single violet eye.

Behaviour: it does not move and does not follow. Facing it is enough: with it anywhere in front of you, one second is too long. Looking at the floor does not help.

Containment: none. Turn your back on it and go back the way you came. Left alone, it goes.

The radiation detector registers it before you see it.
