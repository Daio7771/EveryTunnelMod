EQUIPMENT SHEET - WRENCH AND DETECTOR

Wrench: heavy steel, very resistant. It will outlast anything you hit with it. Slow to swing: make each blow count.

Radiation detector: you already have it on you. It clicks when a Distortion Cloud is within sixteen metres with nothing in between, faster as it comes nearer, until the clicks run into a crackle. A red triangle means it is close.

It registers nothing else.
