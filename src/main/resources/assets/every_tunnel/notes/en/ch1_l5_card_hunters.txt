TECHNICAL SHEET - HUNTERS

Classification: hostile. Mobile. Found in threes.

Description: small, shorter than a person. Black skin, white eyes. They wear lab coats.

Behaviour: they see; they do not hear. When one of them sees you, all three come, fast. One blow from them is light. Three together are not.

Containment: one blow of a heavy tool puts one down. Strike first. Keep a wall at your back. Do not let them surround you.
