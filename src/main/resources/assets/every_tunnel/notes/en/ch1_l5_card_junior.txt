TECHNICAL SHEET - DISTORTION JUNIOR

Classification: not lethal. Stationary.

Description: a small Distortion Cloud, the size of a head, floating at eye level. The same violet eye.

Behaviour: near it, your sight shakes and drifts away from it. While it watches you, the Hunters know where you are.

Containment: hold its eye. Four seconds without looking away and it breaks.

The radiation detector does not register it.
