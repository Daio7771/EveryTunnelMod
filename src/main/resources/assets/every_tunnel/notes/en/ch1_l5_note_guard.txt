NIGHT GUARD NO. 13

If you are reading this, you are the next one. Fourteen, I suppose.

I came down the ladder like you. The hatch shut over me. I went through the water and found the stairs, and the office at the top, and the man behind the desk. He talked for a long time. Then he pointed at the corridor and said there was nothing in there, he promised.

I didn't believe him. I came back down here to think it over. That was a long time ago. The clouds come more often now, and I can't keep food down.

I can't walk any more. You still can. Don't stay down here.

- Night Guard No. 13
