INTERNAL MEMORANDUM - HUMAN RESOURCES

To: All office staff
Subject: Supervision of the reading room

The reading room is supervised at all hours. Supervision is not a member of staff. It is not to be addressed, approached or obstructed.

Its attention does not extend beyond the reading room doors. Staff working in the offices have no cause for concern.

Staff whose duties require them to cross the reading room are to move slowly and keep their lamps off. Supervision sees further towards light, and less of a person who keeps low. No one passes within arm's reach of it unnoticed.

Any member of staff who is noticed is to leave the reading room by the nearest door at once. Do not stop.
