FORMAL COMPLAINT - TO THE MANAGEMENT OFFICE

From: Research Division, staff representative
Subject: Health of staff since the delivery

I write on behalf of the staff of the research levels.

Since the machine was brought down, nothing here has been the same. None of us was told what it is. It is larger than anything we were trained to work with, and it was installed behind doors we are not permitted to open.

Within weeks, staff began to report headaches, nausea and fatigue. Several have since been placed on sick leave, some of them for months.

When I asked what the machine was, I was told that any attempt to investigate it would have consequences, both disciplinary and chemical. I did not ask what was meant by the second.

I request a written answer.
