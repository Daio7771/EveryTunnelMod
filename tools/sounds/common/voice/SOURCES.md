# The director's voice

The one AI exception in the mod's sound (see `docs/en/common/audio.md`): Level 5's director, his twenty lines
in the cinematic (`boss_01` to `boss_20`) and the six phrases he repeats afterwards (`loop_1` to
`loop_6`). ElevenLabs library voices (paid plan, not a cloned voice), generated by the project's author,
cut one file per line and loudness-matched by them (mono Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, -18 LUFS, true peak
-1.5 dBTP). Their text, and the subtitles, are in `../voices.py`.

# The scientist's voice

Also under that exception (AI voices): Chapter 2's prologue, the scientist's four lines (`prologue_01` to
`prologue_04`), from ElevenLabs library voices (paid plan, not a cloned voice) made by the project's author and
handed over as `prol_1.mp3` to `prol_4.mp3`. `../prologue_voices.py` turns them into mono Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, with
one gain for all four (the group at -18 LUFS, true peak under -1.5 dBTP) and keeps their texts and the subtitles.
